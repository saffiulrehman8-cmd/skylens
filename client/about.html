// Platform Features
01

Live Weather Data

Real-time conditions, 10-day forecast, UV Index, AQI breakdown, dew point — all parallel-fetched from open APIs.

02

ECMWF Storm Maps

Wind, rain, temperature, clouds and pressure overlays powered by Windy's European Centre forecast model.

03

Interactive Charts

Switchable 24h trend charts for temperature, humidity, and wind speed with live data from 3-hour intervals.

04

Smart Advisories

Contextual weather alerts — extreme heat, high UV, poor air quality, high winds, low visibility, and more.

05

City Autocomplete

Instant city suggestions as you type, recent searches saved locally, and geolocation on page load.

06

Saved Accounts

Create an account to save up to 10 favorite cities, synced securely to your profile via JWT authentication.
