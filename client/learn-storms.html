Severe
Storms

Physics of lightning, supercells, and tornadic genesis.

Related Topics

Severe thunderstorms form when warm, moisture-laden air rapidly ascends into cooler upper atmosphere. Supercell thunderstorms — the most powerful type — are defined by a persistent deep rotating updraft called a mesocyclone. CAPE values above 2,500 J/kg indicate highly explosive storm potential.

Lightning occurs when electrical charge separation reaches ~3 million V/m. The return stroke channels 30,000A and heats surrounding air to ~30,000K — five times hotter than the sun's surface — in microseconds, producing the thunder shockwave.

⚡ Speed: A lightning bolt travels at up to one-third the speed of light. Thunder delay is ~3 seconds per km distance.
