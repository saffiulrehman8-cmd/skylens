The El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) is Earth's most powerful climate driver on interannual timescales. El Niño: weakened trade winds allow warm water to slosh eastward, driving above-normal rainfall across the southern US while causing drought in Australia.

La Niña: intensified trade winds fuel above-normal rainfall across Southeast Asia and more active Atlantic hurricane seasons.

🌊 Impact Scale: ENSO modulates weather for billions of people and is the dominant source of interannual climate predictability globally.
