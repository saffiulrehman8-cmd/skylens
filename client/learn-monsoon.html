Monsoon
Systems

Seasonal wind reversals that bring life-giving — and destructive — rains.

Monsoons are large-scale seasonal reversals of prevailing winds driven by differential heating between ocean and large landmasses. In summer, continental interiors heat faster, creating a thermal low that draws in moist maritime air — producing torrential rainfall. The South Asian Monsoon delivers 80% of India's annual rainfall from June to September.

Cherrapunji, India, records over 11,000mm annually. Monsoon variability is strongly modulated by ENSO and the Indian Ocean Dipole.

💧 Water Security: The South Asian Monsoon provides water for over 1 billion people and drives agriculture for 600 million farmers.
